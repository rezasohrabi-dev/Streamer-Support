SIPTV Support

IPTV player for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

SIPTV plays the live channels, movies and series from an IPTV subscription you already have. It is a player only: it does not include, sell or recommend any channels or providers.

Need help? Email sohrabi.developer@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version and, if a stream fails, the error message shown by the app. Never send your provider password.

Frequently asked questions

How do I connect to my provider?

Choose Xtream Server and enter the server address, username and password from your provider, then tap Connect — or choose M3U Link and paste the playlist link they gave you. Your details are stored only on your device and are sent only to that server. SIPTV has no accounts of its own: it connects to your provider's server, the same way on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

I don't have a provider yet. Can I try the app?

Yes. Tap No provider yet? Try the demo server on the first screen (or enter server demo, username demo, password demo). It plays public test streams and Creative Commons films by the Blender Foundation, so you can try Live TV, Movies and Series.

A channel shows "Connecting…" and never plays

Most IPTV lines allow one stream at a time. If the same login is playing on another device, wait a few seconds and try again. If it never plays, the stream is down at the provider's side — the app cannot fix a stream the server does not deliver.

A channel shows an error such as HTTP 407, 458 or 461

These codes come from your provider's server: 407 means the account is not allowed to open that channel, 458 means the connection limit is in use, 461/462 means the provider blocked the request. Contact your provider about your subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchases (or tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen) while signed in to the same Apple Account (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or Google Account (Android) that made the purchase.

How do I remove my login?

Settings ▸ Disconnect deletes the saved server details from the device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, iPad and Mac request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, see Google Play refunds.
