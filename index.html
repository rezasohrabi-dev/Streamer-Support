SIPTV Support

IPTV player for iPhone, iPad and Mac

SIPTV plays the live channels, movies and series from an IPTV subscription you already have. It is a player only: it does not include, sell or recommend any channels or providers.

Need help? Email sohrabi.developer@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and, if a stream fails, the error message shown by the app. Never send your provider password.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Choose Xtream Codes and enter the server address, username and password from your provider, or choose M3U and paste the playlist link they gave you. Your details are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud, if enabled) and are sent only to that server.

A channel shows "Connecting…" and never plays

Most IPTV lines allow one stream at a time. If the same login is playing on another device, wait a few seconds and try again. If it never plays, the stream is down at the provider's side — the app cannot fix a stream the server does not deliver.

A channel shows an error such as HTTP 407, 458 or 461

These codes come from your provider's server: 407 means the account is not allowed to open that channel, 458 means the connection limit is in use, 461/462 means the provider blocked the request. Contact your provider about your subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchases (or tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen) while signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I remove my login?

Settings ▸ Sign out deletes the saved server details from the device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
