Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for SIPTV · Last updated: October 14, 2026

SIPTV ("we", "us" or "our") is an IPTV player application for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android published by Mohammad Reza Sohrabi. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used and what we do not do with it.

Information Collection and Use

SIPTV is designed so that we collect nothing about you. We operate no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. The app handles the following data on your device only:

  • Provider login: the server address, username and password (Xtream Codes) or the M3U playlist link that you enter. It is stored on your device and is transmitted only to the server address you entered, in order to load your channel list, artwork and streams.
  • Preferences: pinned channels and categories, preferred audio and subtitle language, aspect ratio and similar settings. Stored on your device.
  • Trial and purchase state: the date your free trial started and whether the app has been unlocked. On iPhone, iPad and Mac this is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud key-value storage so that it follows you between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. On Android it is stored on your device, in Android's backup of app settings to your own Google Account, and in Google Play services Block Store, so that it survives reinstalling the app on the same Google Account. None of it is visible to us.
  • Cached playlist: the channel list returned by your provider is cached on the device so the app opens quickly. It is refreshed from your provider and deleted when you sign out or delete the app.

We do not collect email addresses, names, device identifiers, location, usage data or the content you watch. No data is sold, shared or transferred to us or to any third party. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details.

Android permissions: SIPTV uses the internet and network state to reach your provider and to reconnect when your connection changes; a foreground media-playback service and the notification permission (Android 13 and later) so that a stream can keep playing in the background with a visible “Stop” control; and a wake lock to keep the screen on while video plays. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Third-Party Services

  • Your IPTV provider: when you sign in, the app connects to the server you specified. That provider receives your login and the requests needed to play content, and its own privacy policy governs what it records. SIPTV is not affiliated with any provider.
  • Apple: in-app purchases, Restore Purchases and iCloud key-value storage are provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google: on Android, in-app purchases and restoring them are provided by Google Play Billing, and the backup of the trial state by Android Backup and Google Play services Block Store, under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Demo server: the built-in demo plays public test streams (Apple, Unified Streaming, Mux) and Creative Commons films by the Blender Foundation from those organisations' own servers, which receive the usual network requests needed to deliver a video.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Copyright Notices (DMCA and equivalent laws)

SIPTV does not host or transmit any content; every stream is delivered directly from the server the user configured to the user's device. Notices concerning specific content should therefore be addressed to the operator of that server. If you believe the app itself infringes your rights, or you wish to notify us of a server being used for infringement, email us with:

  • The URL of the stream or server concerned

We will review every valid notice and respond within a reasonable time.

Age Restrictions

SIPTV is rated 18+ on the App Store, is listed for adults (18+) on Google Play and is not directed at children. Because a user's provider may include mature content, the app is intended only for adults, and any parental controls are the responsibility of the account holder.

Your Choices

  • Delete your data: Settings ▸ Disconnect removes the saved login from the device; deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. To delete the iCloud copy of your trial/purchase state, remove the app's data under iOS Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage. On Android, app backups can be deleted under Google Drive ▸ Backups or in your Google Account settings.
  • Restore purchases: available in the app at any time; handled by Apple or Google Play.
